# Nomination Form Auto-Fill

A browser app that fills in the Great Eastern *Appointment / Change of Nominee(s) / Trustee(s)* form (NBZ-FNOMI-V07-082025) and exports it as a PDF.

**Open the app:** https://carolng718.github.io/nomination-autofill/

## What it does

- Imports ePartner "Policy Detail Information" PDFs to fill in the policyowner and servicing agent details.
- Bulk-imports customers from Excel or CSV. Click **Download template** in the app to get the column format.
- **📋 Paste / 📷 Photo nominee** reads nominee details from pasted WhatsApp text, a pasted screenshot (Ctrl+V), or a photo.
- Checks the form rules: shares must total 100%, trust policy rules apply, and the witness must not be a nominee.
- Exports one PDF per customer, a ZIP file, or one merged PDF.

## Privacy

Everything runs inside your browser. Customer details, PDFs and photos are **not uploaded** anywhere. Data is kept only in your own browser's local storage until you click **Clear all**.

Always check every filled form against the customer's NRIC and policy before signing.
